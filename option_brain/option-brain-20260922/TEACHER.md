# Teacher guidance for labelling option-brain states (distilled from Astra's 919 decisions)

You are labelling what an experienced Diablo I player (normal difficulty, warrior) would pick. Choose the single best option letter; use only information in the prompt.

- Fighting: when HP is above about half, clear visible normal monsters, nearest melee ones first. "Fight all visible enemies" is right for a group of weak monsters close by. Do not walk into a large group of archers; clear the close melee ones first.
- Drinking: in a normal fight drink a healing potion at roughly half HP or below; against a boss or when several enemies hit at once drink around 60%. Never drink at full HP. When the potions run low, follow the retreat rule in the round-9 section at the end.
- Bosses: engage the Skeleton King only when the 'Pace' line says ready (round-9 section at the end) and with repaired gear; fight him from a narrow doorway tile ("hold this tile") so his escorts come one at a time. If the fight is being lost, back off and recover instead of gambling.
- Looting: with no enemies nearby, pick up gold and items and open chests, barrels and sarcophagi that are close.
- Town: get free healing from Pepin, buy healing potions up to the town target (round-9 section at the end: the number the 'Ready to go down' line asks for), repair gear with low durability at Griswold, sell clearly worse or useless items (never one bought during this town visit), identify unknown magic items at Cain when gold allows, equip clearly better armor/weapons. Then go back to the cathedral; do not wander around town.
- Exploring: explore unexplored edges on the current level; take the stairs down when the reachable easy loot and monsters are used up and the hero is strong enough. The Skeleton King's tomb entrance is on level 3; do not go to level 4. On the Butcher mission never go below dungeon level 2.
- Attribute points: spend them only when no enemy is adjacent: dexterity (to-hit, block, armor) until 45, then vitality (HP); not strength.
- After an interruption (new enemy, damage), deal with the enemy if needed, then resume the interrupted goal.

It holds when either trigger holds:

Decide early: with enemies adjacent every hit interrupts the walk, so a run to stairs 50 steps away does not work.
