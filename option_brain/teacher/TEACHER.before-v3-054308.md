# Teacher guidance for labelling option-brain states (distilled from Astra's 919 decisions)

You are labelling what an experienced Diablo I player (normal difficulty, warrior) would pick. Choose the single best option letter; use only information in the prompt.

- Fighting: when HP is above about half, clear visible normal monsters, nearest melee ones first. "Fight all visible enemies" is right for a group of weak monsters close by. Do not walk into a large group of archers; clear the close melee ones first.
- Drinking: in a normal fight drink a healing potion at roughly half HP or below; against a boss or when several enemies hit at once drink around 60%. Never drink at full HP. If no potions and HP is low, back off or leave the level.
- Bosses: engage the Skeleton King only at level 6-7 or higher with 6+ healing potions and repaired gear; fight him from a narrow doorway tile ("hold this tile") so his escorts come one at a time. If the fight is being lost, back off and recover instead of gambling.
- Looting: with no enemies nearby, pick up gold and items and open chests, barrels and sarcophagi that are close.
- Town: get free healing from Pepin, buy healing potions until about 6 in belt+pack, repair gear with low durability at Griswold, sell clearly worse or useless items, identify unknown magic items at Cain when gold allows, equip clearly better armor/weapons. Then go back to the cathedral; do not wander around town.
- Exploring: explore unexplored edges on the current level; take the stairs down when the reachable easy loot and monsters are used up and the hero is strong enough. The Skeleton King's tomb entrance is on level 3; do not go to level 4.
- Attribute points: dexterity (to-hit, block, armor) or strength (damage).
- After an interruption (new enemy, damage), deal with the enemy if needed, then resume the interrupted goal.

Added during the night of 2026-09-22 (seen in full games with the new hands):

- Skeleton King fight, learned from the local student's first tomb fights (2026-09-23): at character level 6 with 0 potions, a sword and 20 dexterity it hit the King zero times in 8 attacks and died; another hero reached the tomb at level 7 with 1493 unspent gold and no potions and died. The engine rules: chance to hit = character level + dexterity/2 + weapon bonus + class base, minus the monster's armour (the King's armour is high); against undead (the King and all his skeletons) a sword does HALF damage and a club/mace/morning star/flail does 50% MORE. So: before the tomb, go to town, buy healing potions until 10 or more are carried (gold is useless in the tomb), repair gear, and prefer a club or mace for this fight if one is available; put attribute points into dexterity (to hit him) rather than strength until about 40 dexterity; fight the escorts from a doorway or corridor; fight the King himself only at character level 7+ with 8+ potions; if potions drop to 3, walk out of the tomb, restock in town, and come back.
- The Butcher (dungeon level 2, 110 HP, regenerates, hits hard) cannot be beaten by standing and trading blows at character level 5-6. On the Skeleton King mission leave him alone: do not open the door of his room, and if he appears, walk away toward the stairs up and leave the level. Only fight him with a big level advantage and many potions, from a doorway or the stairs.
