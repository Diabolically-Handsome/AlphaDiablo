## 3. Reading

1. **The channel really does save lives**: paired on the same seeds, the full channel keeps 8 more episodes
   alive than no channel (12 saved / 4 lost), deaths 22 → 14; this is the first significant survival
   improvement seen in a 48-seed pairing (UCB95 < 0).
2. **But half of the saving comes from "descending less and later"**: L2 reached 24 → 20, steps on L2 351 →
   192; the per-step hazard fell only 15%, short of the 30% the pre-registration requires. Characters that
   reach L2 still fight at AC 7-9 (the dead average AC 7.3), with an L2 roster of 100+ monsters: this is
   R14's skill wall, not a price wall.
3. **Money is the binding constraint (the objection recorded on 2026-09-04 holds)**: 41/48 episodes went to
   town, yet only 10 descents met the six-condition law; final gold 81, while one chest armour costs 200.
   The worker neither picks up nor sells gear, so it goes to town with the one or two hundred gold it picked
   up and cannot afford both armour and four potions. Arm (a) picked up gold with nowhere to spend it (120
   gold) and had the worst survival.
4. (a) being worse than A0′ (20 vs 26 survived) is at the edge of noise (L2 deaths 21 vs 15, both with 24
   arrivals); another explanation is that under coach-v03, with no potions to buy, the worker is never
   "ready", so every descent is pushed out through the forced 3600-step exit; this is left for T0′ to
   re-check and is not a conclusion.
5. The certified worker's A0′ again shows that it is an L1 camper (L2 reached 3/48), consistent with the R16
   verdict.
