## 2. Three findings of the day (three layers drilled through in one day)
1. **The real escape hatch is in the frozen mask rule**: at options_env:700-702 the exhausted flag forces
   m[FARM]=False and hands control to DIVE, so every "want" of the coach is overridden by the mask
   fallback. Arm b's bit-for-bit copy is hard evidence.
2. **Enforcement on the wage side works**: amendment 2 (an unqualified descent earns nothing) pulls the idle
   exploitation out by the roots (kills 11→31, extraction restored), with zero leaks in the refusals (G0-9
   and every arm's accounts exact).
3. **The decisive number: 0 vested / 15,459 denied.** In a whole round of 327k training steps and 636
   descents, **not a single one reached the first rung of the readiness table** (the L2 threshold clvl 3 /
   HP 90 / AC 15 / dmg 8). Constant across arms and managers: the growth ceiling within 3000 steps is
   clvl≈1.5-1.7, AC≈5-7; even clearing L1 completely (98 monsters) reaches only clvl 2-3. **The thresholds of
   the human strategy tables are reached through accumulation across levels + shopping in town + picking up
   all the gear; in the engine these are either missing (shopping is not in the action space, and 60-80
   gold piles up with nothing to spend it on) or inefficient (gear pick-ups are rare).**

## 3. Overall verdict

core: **classroom (R12) → willingness (R13) → pricing (R14) → qualification (R15) → growth engine
