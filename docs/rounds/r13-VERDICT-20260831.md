## 3. Scientific reading (the case's three main outputs)
1. **The wall really came down**: the structural law pinned down in R12 (the worker's weights are

   **vindicated** as a mechanism.
2. **The worker really learned to survive at depth**: paired saved:lost of 71:1/76:1 is not noise but an
   overwhelming behavioural change; this is the first time anyone has passed gate A since it was set.
3. **The incentive vacuum materialised exactly (the pre-registered main scientific risk)**: the survival
   skill the worker learned = **refusing to dive**. Under the wage-stripping rule, stepping onto stairs
   earns nothing, while clearing monsters inside DIVE windows collects the B depth multiplier (kills on the
   devil sheets rose by 59%/64%), and death is a negative term: the rational optimum of the pay slip is
   "clear monsters safely in the deep-water classroom, never step onto the stairs, never die". During
   training the a11 prior kept descent exploration at 1.76 per 10 windows, but the learned argmax pushed the
   prior back down. **The worker learns what the economy teaches: there is no descent pay on the v2 pay
   slip, so the worker never descends.** This is not failed learning; it is perfect obedience to the
   current wage rules.
