# R15 pre-registration: readiness-gate coach (readiness-v1), phase 1
Base: the clauses of the whole chain R13/R13.2/R14/R14.2 (+ the option E appendix) are inherited.
Decision of 2026-09-01: research published strategy guides, combine them into a single power value, and
let the manager allow the next level only once the character is above that value. The goal is to be the
first AI to complete the game, not to speed-run it.

## 1. Rationale
The R14 four-arm run showed that gate B is stuck at a skill wall (h(2)=0.55 / h(3)=0.67); root cause = the
current coach level-margin-1 lets the worker through on a single dimension, and from d3 on it lets it go
1-9 levels early: sitting the exam unequipped.
This case loads a "readiness table by level", distilled from thirty years of strategy guides
([r15-READINESS-TABLE-draft.md](r15-READINESS-TABLE-draft.md), five research lanes cross-checked: Jarulf +
monstdat + community guides), into the manager: **weakest-link logic, no passage while any dimension is
below the bar**. The project goal is formally re-tuned: completion > speed run.

## 2. Rules
- New coach `manager_heuristic="readiness-v1"` (the named set in worker_env is extended; still mutually
  exclusive with npz; the contract field manager_heuristic is recorded as usual; the
  --allow-manager-change path already exists): `power_ratio(raw, dlvl+1) ≥ 1.0` lets DIVE through,
  otherwise FARM; an exhausted level is the escape hatch (exhausted = readiness cannot improve, so go down
  on a best-effort basis);
- `readiness_power_ratio` = min(clvl/C, maxHP/H, AC/A, (item_max_damage+damage_mod)/D[, fire_resist/FR
  (d≥13), magic_resist/MR (d≥15)]); the 16-level lower bounds of the four tables C/H/A/D and the
  resistance gates come from readiness table v0.1 (module constants in worker_env; field names verified by
  the live probe probe_r15_raw);
- The wage rule keeps the best pieces of R14.2+R14.3: escrow 0.5 × the d^1.6 convex curve + the a11 lever
  2.0 (the option E combination, which with the readiness gate forms a "qualification - pay - insurance"
  trio);
- Measuring the deployed form: **a forensic probe**
  [probe_r15_deployment.py](../../train/runs/r10-staging/probe_r15_deployment.py) (readiness scripted
  manager × worker zip, double registration, economy v2, 3000 steps): not an exam sheet, it does not go
  through the archive identity system, and its results go only into the ledger; a formal "exam rule for
  heuristic managers + a long campaign protocol" is listed as a phase-2 amendment.

## 5. Risks
- Escape-hatch dilution: once a small level runs out of monsters, the exhausted escape still lets an
  unqualified dive through (the same dilemma as a human player); the readiness gate guarantees "not going
  down early", not "always qualified";
- The AC table is based on human gearing pace, and our a14 pick-up pace may not keep up: the AC dimension
  may become a long-term weak link that makes the coach FARM too much; audit the per-dimension blocking
  distribution (forensic item);
- The meaning of damage_mod (whether it includes the strength growth bonus) is inferred from consistency on
  the starting frame; drift after levelling needs re-checking by in-arm forensics;
- Within a 3000-step budget the readiness pace (fully training each level) may never reach depth; this case
  measures "the net effect of the gate under the same budget", and the push for completion belongs to the
  phase-2 long protocol.
