## 2. Rules (option D, in the policy_reward layer; the W ledger / identity / manager ledger untouched)
- `worker_descend_escrow_fraction = 0.5`: the descent fee for a new deepest level × 0.5 goes into escrow
  (0.5×48×Σlevel numbers; only new deepest levels count, immune to cycles);
- **Vest**: paid in full into policy_reward at the next non-death window closure (a timeout truncation =
  alive, paid as usual; a no-progress death counts as a non-death closure and vests; forfeiture only on a
  real death);
- **Forfeit**: any real death (in a live window or a fast-forward segment) forfeits everything: a
  worker-side transplant of the forfeiting anti-suicide valve certified in R11. The essential difference
  from R11's manager-side collapse: the worker fully controls its own survival (option B 61/63, option A
  13/21 as proof), so escrow is a learnable contract;
- Mutually exclusive with option B (fail-closed); option B's fraction set to zero and shelved; the a11
  lever 2.0 kept.

## Appendix: R14.3 option E (request of 2026-08-31: find a usable pay curve)
### Measured risk curve (the self-driven exam sheets of the option B / option A / R13 arms combined, n=1280 episodes)
| d | reached | died on this level | hazard |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1280 | 205 | 0.160 |
| 2 | 350 | 192 | 0.549 |
| 3 | 72 | 48 | 0.667 |
| 4 | 8 | 5 | 0.625 |
Geometric fit: **h(d) ≈ 0.23 × 1.53^(d−1)**. Risk rises geometrically, so a linear wage must stall at the
break-even level (measured: stuck at L2); the view that the curve must rise with difficulty is confirmed by
the data.
### Curve rule
The escrow price per level becomes **fraction × unit × d^power**, with a fitted suggestion of
**power=1.6** (working back from the EV target, pay(d)≈36×d^1.57; p=1.0 is bit-identical to the old
rule). On levels with h>0.5 no finite price breaks even immediately; the curve's job is **to pay tuition**:
get the worker to fight on L3, and once skill lowers h, levels unlock one by one (the death rate falling
from 97% to 10% has already shown that h is a function of the policy).
### Ladder
arm-c (linear ×2) goes first to isolate the dose axis; if gate B still falls short → arm-d with
--worker-descend-escrow-power 1.6 (the knob is implemented, default 1.0 with zero drift, test file 8/8; a
direct-drive settlement check verified d^1.6 per-level pricing and exact death forfeiture).
