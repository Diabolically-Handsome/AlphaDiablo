## 0. Construction record: the first probe "bought 32 scrolls, cast 60 times, opened no portal"

The first probe was valid but never opened a portal. Traced layer by layer: three root causes and two seams, all fixed on the Python side; engine and bridge unchanged:
1. The `wait` command means "cancel the current action" in the engine (player.Stop, standing still in the middle of the casting animation), and the script used wait to wait for the portal, killing the cast it had just queued → a new `hold` command (only advances the engine, cancels nothing);
2. The generic closing fence after every action also cancels the intent → an accepted cast is first advanced until the portal appears or the animation returns to standing (at most 16 ticks), the same as opening doors (open);
3. Being hit by an adjacent monster interrupts reading the scroll → casting only happens with no monster within 2 tiles; otherwise it walks along the retreat route toward the up stairs and hands back if no gap appears within 60 ticks (the stairs retreat takes over);
4. The town service never handed control back in town, so buying scrolls from the witch had no place → buying is inserted before the "return" stage of the town trip (decide first, then command, leaving no dangling receipt) and booked in the loot-selling ledger;
5. After arriving in town through the portal, the existing town flow (healing, potions, selling gear) did not connect, and the script went back to the portal after 6 ticks → arriving in town through the portal now enters the existing flow at the "outbound" stage (skipping the gold-pickup stage that belongs only to L1),
   and when the flow reaches the return stage it closes the town service and hands over to the portal's return leg (decide first, then command).
Smoke run after the fixes (seed 2133010): two complete cycles: buy scrolls → open a portal at HP 36 → healed to 86 in town → back through the town-side portal to the same spot on level 2 → … → second round HP 35 → 94, survived until the clock ran out.
The re-run probe's control-arm rows are bit-identical to the R18-G control arm (minus the two None keys added to the probe later; the driver first misjudged this as VOID, which was corrected and recorded). **The probe is valid (REPORT_ONLY).**

## 2. Verdict

- H0 L2 arrival ✓ (35 vs 37, not significantly lower; buying scrolls lengthens the first town trip, and 25 games' trajectories before the first descent differ because the random stream shifted; the pre-registration was changed to report-only for this);
- H1 hazard ✓: 0.370 → 0.266 (−28%);
- H2 paired survival ✗: net +2, UCB95 +0.056;
- H3 share dying during retreat ✓: 24% vs 25% (barely);
- H4 descending again and occupancy: descending again 23 vs 25 games (✗), but L2+ occupancy after going up 0.29 → 0.46 (the portal cuts the round-trip time of "retreat → recover → descend again" to the minimum);
- Also: L3 arrivals 4 vs 2; survivors staying on L2 6 vs 4.

## 3. Reading

The portal did what it should: retreats no longer walk, recovery lands back at the same spot, the share of time on level 2 rises sharply and the hazard falls by almost 30%. But it did not significantly change who survives,
nor did it clearly make progress (L3 4 vs 2 is within noise). The reason is the same as in R18-C: back on level 2 the worker faces the same crowd and is the same worker that cannot win;
the portal makes the cycle faster, not stronger. Moreover the 200-gold scroll spends the money (final gold 216 → 71); the cost of being unable to afford armor and potions already shows across the 48 games.
