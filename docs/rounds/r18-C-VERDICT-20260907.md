## 2. The cycle turns

- 25 games of the retreat arm **went down to L2 again** after going upstairs (0 in the control arm by definition); 10 of them survived until the clock ran out.
- Successful retreats per game: 0 in 23 games, 1 in 4, 2 in 8, **3 in 13** (3 = the engine cap; 13 games used up the allowance).
- 25 second attempts, 21 reaching the upper level (84%); about 79 attempts in all, 59 reaching the upper level (75%) and 20 dying on the way (25%, consistent with T0″'s 28%).
- After the first trip upstairs, the median share of time spent on L2+ is **28.7%**; the rest is recovery on L1 and in town; only 4 games spent more than half their time on L2+.
- Control arm without retreat: of those who reached L2, **none survived 9000 ticks** (0/36); with retreat: 9/35.

## 3. But no progress

- The maximum depth distribution is identical in both arms (L2:35, L1:11, L3:2). Retreat lets the character live longer, not go deeper.
- Mean clvl 3.23 vs 3.27, kills 118 vs 115: the cycle does not make the character stronger. Experience on L2 comes too slowly, and healing and buying potions in town do not raise the level.
- The 13 games that used up all 3 retreats still died on L2; in other words, retreat **buys time**, not **victory**. Every time the cycle returns to L2 it faces the same 130-odd monsters
  and the same way of pulling 7–8 at a time (T0″: on average 7.5 monsters within 6 tiles when retreat triggers).

## 4. Answer to the question

"Is there progress": **there is a cycle, but no progress.** Retreat pushes the L2 hazard per 1000 ticks from 0.72 down to 0.37 and turns "reaching L2 means certain death" into "can last 9000 ticks",
but the character cannot win fights on L2 and can only fall back again and again. The bottleneck has moved from "cannot retreat" to "cannot win", which is exactly the four points raised in the design review:
an aggro cap, target selection, the Town Portal scroll and positioning. The first two decide whether a fight on L2 can be won; the scroll decides the cost of retreating; positioning has to be trained.

## 5. Next steps (all four points to be fixed)

1. R18-D aggro cap: stop pulling monsters once ≥ N live monsters are nearby, finish them in place or fall back to the stairs (a mask rule, testable with zero training);
2. R18-E target selection: target priority from a threat table × HP (a controller-level script, testable with zero training);
3. R18-B: wire retreat into the training windows and receipts (positioning can only be trained, and this is the prerequisite);
4. R18-F Town Portal scroll: engine gate + buying the scroll + casting + the landing spot on return (the largest engineering effort).
The retreat cap of 3 and the retreat rule 50%/75% are not changed in this round (a change means a new pre-registration).
