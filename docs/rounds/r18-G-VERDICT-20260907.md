## 2. Verdict

- H1 hazard ✓: 0.370 → 0.228 (−38%), the largest drop of all zero-training interventions in this round.
- H3 live monsters nearby at the trigger ✓: 6.14 → 5.45: **the pulling mechanism really is the whole-map hunt**; the suspicion that "it aggroes a whole level at once" is confirmed at the mechanism level.
- H4 kill efficiency fell as expected (13.1 → 8.9): pulling fewer = killing fewer.
- H2 paired survival ✗: net +4, UCB95 +0.035; 48 pairs cannot separate it; the direction is right, the size is not enough.
- No progress: the depth distribution is unchanged; but games "alive and still on level 2" went from 4 to 9, the L2+ occupancy share 0.29 → 0.37, games descending again 25 → 30.

## 3. Reading

Of the three level-2 rules, only the one that touches **the pulling mechanism itself** has an effect; the aggro cap, which only acts once the crowd is already nearby, does not.
With hunting off, the worker only explores locally on level 2: it pulls fewer monsters, lives longer and spends more time on level 2, at the cost of killing more slowly. It did not make the character stronger, and so it did not go deeper either;
that is still a matter for training. Recommendation: hunt_scope=l1-only goes into training arm v1 (as a world setting; training decides whether the worker can gain experience at the slower pace).
