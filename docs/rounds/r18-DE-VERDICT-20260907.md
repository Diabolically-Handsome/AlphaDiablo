## 2. Verdict: H1–H4 all fail (a null result)

- H1 hazard: A 0.404 and AB 0.371, neither below ctl 0.370.
- H2 paired survival: AB net −2 (UCB95 +0.151); A net −1; B net −3 (UCB95 +0.165). The direction is opposite to the hypothesis, but all within noise.
- H3 live monsters nearby at the trigger: A 6.18 vs ctl 6.14: **the aggro cap did not reduce the monsters present at retreat time**.
- H4 kill efficiency: B 11.8 and AB 11.3, **below** ctl 13.1: target selection made the worker fight more slowly.
- H5 depth: L3 arrivals 2/3/3/2, noise.

## 3. Why it did not help (an honest mechanism reading)

1. **The aggro cap treats the symptom, not the mechanism.** Monster crowds form when a10's whole-map hunt drags the character into rooms while the window has no monsters; the cap masks a10 only after the crowd is already next to the character,
   when "holding still" and "exploring on" give about the same result; the almost unchanged monster count at retreat time (6.18 vs 6.14) is the evidence. The real pulling mechanism (whole-map hunting,
   pathing through doors) was not touched at all. This was the last risk a reviewer listed during the survey, and the data confirmed it.
2. **Target selection makes the worker walk further.** Ranged first means crossing melee monsters to hit a distant archer; a9 only has a 10-tick budget, walking eats the budget, and kill efficiency drops from 13.1 to 11.8.
   The worker is frozen; it does not know the target changed and keeps attacking out of old habit.
3. **Both rules were added to the "hands", and the hands are frozen.** Without training the worker will not learn to exploit "holding" or "ranged first". A zero-training probe can only test the rule itself,
   and what it measured is that "the rule by itself does not save lives"; it cannot rule out that it helps after training.

## 4. Answer to the four points

- Aggro cap v1: built, certified, tested: **ineffective**. A real fix would have to change whole-map hunting on L2 (the pulling mechanism), which is a new zero-training probe (R18-G: hunting off on L2).
- Target selection v1: built, certified, tested: **ineffective, slightly negative**. The interface is kept; the thresholds and ranking are not changed in this round.
- Positioning: the training-window wiring (R18-B) is complete and passed 125 + 2101 tests; the draft launch order is summarized in the R18-B launch evidence ([r18-B-LAUNCH-EVIDENCE-20260907.md](r18-B-LAUNCH-EVIDENCE-20260907.md)).
