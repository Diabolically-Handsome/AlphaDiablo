## 3. Structural causes of death (the case's main scientific output)
1. **Classroom ceiling**: the fast-forward architecture of WorkerWindowEnv fast-forwards every non-FARM
   window (DIVE/RESUPPLY), so they never enter the gradient: the classroom is structurally open only in
   FARM windows. The SMART coach can move the FARM classroom to deeper levels (sentinel: 797 fast-forward
   dives), but **it can never turn a DIVE window itself into a classroom**.
2. **Trajectory-tube immunity in the devil domain**: in evaluation the devil manager chooses DIVE from the
   start, and every visited state falls inside the old worker's deterministic trajectory tube; neither
   arm's fine-tuning flipped the argmax of any step in that tube → all three are bit-identical. The coach
   is irrelevant: this is the third independent replication of the phenomenon, and it is now pinned down
   as a structural law.
3. **Learning really happened, but in the wrong place**: in the M29 domain, arm 1's l3+ nearly doubled
   (23/32 vs 14/17), and arm 2 rose in pool b too (30), which proves the worker did learn more diving inside
   FARM windows; the price was −20% shallow combat strength (arm 1) to −25/−35% (arm 2). The deeper the
   classroom, the heavier the forgetting (the difference between arms in pool a is significant).

## Addendum 1 (2026-08-30, mechanism correction after the R13 opening survey)
The mechanism stated in section 3, item 2, "neither arm's fine-tuning flipped the argmax of any step in
that tube", is **wrong**; it is corrected here and kept on record:

**The real mechanism: the steering wheel of a DIVE window was never in the worker's hands. The worker's
argmax was never consulted, rather than "consulted but not flipped".**
Three pieces of hard code evidence (found in the R13 opening survey and checked personally against the
master copy):
1. `options_env.py:1704`: `OptionsEnv.step(option)` looks up `self._workers` by option, and if nothing is
   found it runs the frozen script's inner loop `dispatch(mode,...)` (:1731+) for the whole window;
2. `worker_env.py:688-694`: in training, WorkerWindowEnv **never registers workers** when it builds
   OptionsEnv, so every fast-forward window (DIVE/RESUPPLY) is driven by the script and is structurally
   unrelated to the worker's weights;
3. `eval_assembled.py:1179/1190`: in evaluation, `load_worker` returns only `{FARM: callback}`, so the DIVE
   windows on the exam sheet **are driven by the script as well**.

Corollary: the devil manager always issues DIVE → the worker never produces a single action during the
whole exam → the trajectory is 100% determined by the script → the three-way bit identity is a **trivial
necessity**, not "fine-tuning was not strong enough". R12's classroom-ceiling conclusion (section 3, item
1) stands and is strengthened: not only is the classroom closed in DIVE windows, so is the exam hall. The
reform must hand over the driving to the worker on both the training and the exam side; this is the
foundation of the R13 pre-registration (r13-PREREG-DRAFT-v0.1, identical to
[r13-PREREG-FROZEN-20260830.md](r13-PREREG-FROZEN-20260830.md)).
