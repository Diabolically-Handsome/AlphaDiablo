# FORENSICS: manager inquiry, "is the manager mechanism itself the problem?"

2026-07-18. Four-seat panel (chronicle / case history / defence / verdict), merged with the P7
intervention replay. The question under review: the project appeared to stall after the manager was
introduced. Read-only throughout (except P7, which was scheduled separately).

## Verdicts by item

- **V1: the coupling fragility of a frozen manager × a continually trained worker is a structural
  defect: upheld** (what is found at fault is "the frozen examiner as a permanent post", not the
  hierarchical mechanism; the general claim is reserved until P8).
- **V2: H should no longer be the launch judge: partly upheld.** "The observation design failed" is too
  strong (the 8 gauge dimensions carry the information and are learnable, and M29 learned a robust
  boundary from the same observation; the real gaps = no robustness constraint in training + the
  manager's extras lack an exhausted flag, a registered mechanism candidate for R-M FAIL 0.6478). The
  "launch monopoly" is upheld (the R1-W7 contract fixes DEFAULT_MANAGER; depth-2 exposure is 7 under H vs
  15 under M29, and three cases on record differ by 19-33 points with the same worker). Past verdicts
  under H remain valid as "end-to-end readings of the H assembled agent"; what fails is only reading them
  as "worker capability".
- **V3: the manager mechanism caused the stall: causation not upheld; the phenomenon depends on the
  scale.** On the gold-pool / title scale the stall is real (no gold spent after v24-golden, zero
  launches in the eight campaigns v25-v32); on the science scale there was +52% in two days
  (92.0→140.3), the depth economy was unlocked, and the v3 science anchor of 152.8 is the highest in the
  project's history. The real lesions are three: the frozen H monopolises the launch decision; the gate
  lines were not recalibrated for v3 (18/32 over four draws 11→16→17; the death gate struck down the mean
  winner three times in a row, and the v31 verdict openly admits it is "systematically too tight"); and
  the measurement break of the R1 world re-anchoring (the zero-change ctrl also shows −20.74, unrelated to
  the manager).
- **V4: abolish the manager and return to a flat policy: not advisable, the evidence is one-sided** (flat
  policies were eliminated empirically: two local optima, the stair sprinter and the honest refuser; the
  oracle falsification led to the hierarchy; the first v22 campaign scored 93.9 vs 80.2; 100% of the depth
  execution channel is the manager's D option; an independent peer project converged on the same
  architecture).
- **V5: manager reform case: opened**, executed in menu order; P8 is the prerequisite for any final
  attribution.

## Reform menu (executed in order)

1. **Make bundled two-manager evaluation standard** (low cost, high return; the F2 specification is
   ready; merged into the pre-registration of the 4 × 2 bundled evaluation case, awaiting only a launch
   decision);
2. **Dead-gate recalibration + a mid-training canary evaluation** (the current calib gate never fired, so
   any recalibration is a net improvement; the canary seed set must be pre-registered);
3. **A separate case for recalibrating the gate lines under v3** (measurement-case specification,
   two-sided test, with a no-retroactivity clause written in);
4. **Change the launch judge through an anchor bridge** (prerequisite = data from two or three cases of
   item 1; the old anchor's original values are sealed, with a dual-reading transition; a
   constitution-level change, and bumping the contract field needs its own decision; note that M29 shares
   H's exhausted blind spot and the 7017-type window-choice blind spot, so a change of judge is not the
   end point, and bundling stays standard, never returning to a single examiner);
5. **Add an exhausted flag to the manager observation + a robustness constraint + fresh re-education**
   (a contract change to v3.1, linked to the course-5 dry-window course and P8);
6. **Joint training: postponed** (the longest chain of prerequisites, and it destroys forensic
   identifiability; not eligible to open before P8 and items 1-3 are in place).

## Key points merged from P7

The workers (including sov) can execute dives intact (sov case B 8/8); forcing D gains +40 to +53 on the
legs on average (median +1.4/+20.2), and the disaster group wins 26/38: most of the loss is at the level
of window choice and can be recovered. The residual narrows to sov@7017 (a harvesting defect of about
−120) and part of 7013. New finding: a "window-choice blind spot shared by both managers" (ctrl@7017).
Forced diving carries a death tail (7 deaths in 75 episodes), so the throne's restraint has a price
basis. See the P7 appendix of [FORENSICS-F2](FORENSICS-F2-retraining-regression.md).

## Limitations

F-lock has effective replication ≈1 (P8 pending); the disaster list was selected after the fact and is
horizon-sensitive; the compensating-manager hypothesis is not excluded; "any continued training must
collapse" is still an extrapolation without evidence.
